About SmartSleep

SmartSleep is an Apple Watch-exclusive sleep tracking and smart alarm application that helps you wake up refreshed by detecting your optimal wake-up moment during light sleep.

  • Automatic sleep detection using heart rate and motion sensors
  • Smart alarm that wakes you during light sleep for refreshing mornings
  • Detailed sleep analysis with daily scores (0-100)
  • Sleep stage tracking (Deep Sleep, Light Sleep, Awake)
  • Weekly and monthly sleep statistics
  • Full HealthKit integration

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the smart alarm work?

SmartSleep monitors your sleep stages using heart rate and motion data. Within 15 minutes before your set alarm time, it detects when you're in light sleep and gently wakes you with haptic feedback for the most refreshing wake-up experience.

Do I need to wear my Apple Watch while sleeping?

Yes, SmartSleep requires you to wear your Apple Watch during sleep to track your heart rate, movement, and sleep stages for accurate analysis and smart alarm functionality.

Is my health data secure?

Absolutely. All your health data is stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers. SmartSleep respects your privacy completely.

Which Apple Watch models are supported?

SmartSleep supports Apple Watch Series 4 and later running watchOS 10.0 or newer.

How is the sleep score calculated?

Your sleep score (0-100) is calculated based on total sleep duration, deep sleep percentage, number of awakenings, and heart rate variability data.

# MERGE ARENA - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 5, 2026**

## About the App

MERGE ARENA is a number merge puzzle game where you combine matching numbers to achieve high scores and compete with players worldwide.

## Information We Collect

### Game Data (Stored Locally Only)

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to external servers:

- Game scores and high scores
- Achievement progress
- Daily challenge completion status
- Ranked match statistics and rank
- In-game currency (coins)
- Unlocked themes
- Energy system data

**Important:** All game data is stored locally using UserDefaults and is never sent to any external server. Your game progress is private and remains on your device.

### Advertising Data

This app uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. AdMob may collect the following information:

- Device information (model, operating system, advertising identifier)
- IP address
- Advertisement viewing and interaction data
- Approximate location (country/region)

For more information about how Google uses data, please see:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## How We Use Information

### Game Data
- Save your game progress
- Track achievements and rewards
- Display statistics and leaderboards
- Manage the energy system

### Advertising Data
- Display personalized advertisements
- Improve ad relevance
- Measure ad performance

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the app. AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to show you personalized ads based on your interests.

**Types of Ads:**
- Banner ads (displayed on main menu)
- Interstitial ads (displayed between games)
- Rewarded video ads (optional - view to earn energy or unlock themes)

You can control ad personalization through your device settings:
- iOS: Settings → Privacy → Advertising → Limit Ad Tracking

## Data Security

- All game data is stored locally on your device
- We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information
- No user accounts or registration required
- No external servers involved

## Children's Privacy

MERGE ARENA is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All data is stored locally, and we comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).
